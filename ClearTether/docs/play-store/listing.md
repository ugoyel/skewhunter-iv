**Full description:**

```
ClearTether turns your Android phone into a private Wi-Fi network for your own devices: your work laptop, MacBook, iPad, iPhone or another Android phone.

ONE SWITCH
Tap the power button. ClearTether creates a secure WPA2 Wi-Fi Direct network and starts sharing.

CONNECT IN SECONDS
• iPhone & Android: scan the QR code with the camera
• Android: tap the phones together (NFC)
• Windows, Mac & Linux: open the built-in setup page for one-tap configuration

ZERO SETUP ON ANDROID
Install ClearTether on the other phone and choose "Use internet". Every app works automatically, with no proxy settings.

WORK-LAPTOP FRIENDLY
HTTPS traffic passes through untouched, so certificates, SSL pinning and company security tools keep working.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Traffic goes straight from your phone to the sites your devices visit. ClearTether has no servers in between.

FREE AND PREMIUM
• Free: 30 minutes of sharing every day
• Premium: unlimited sharing on every device. Monthly or yearly, with a free trial on the yearly plan. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Wi-Fi must be turned on (it doesn't need to be connected to a network).
• "Use internet" mode on Android uses Android's VPN service only to route that device's apps to your own ClearTether phone. No traffic is sent to the developer.
• Check that your mobile plan allows sharing your connection with your other devices.
```
