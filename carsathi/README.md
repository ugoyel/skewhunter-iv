# carsathi: carpooling for India

carsathi is a BlaBlaCar-style Android app for sharing rides between cities. Drivers publish the empty seats in their car and passengers book them, so everyone splits the cost of fuel and tolls. The colours follow BlaBlaCar: bright blue `#00AFF5` for actions and dark petrol `#054752` for text.

**Download:** [carsathi.apk (latest build)](https://github.com/ugoyel/skewhunter-iv/releases/download/carsathi-latest/carsathi.apk). It needs Android 8.0 or later. Open the downloaded file on your phone and allow "install unknown apps" when Android asks.

**Demo account:** `demo@carsathi.in` / `carsathi123`. You can also tap *Explore with the demo account* on the welcome screen, or sign up with your own email.

## Features

| Area | What's included |
|---|---|
| **Sign up / login** | Step-by-step sign-up: email, name, date of birth (18+ check), title, password rules, phone with OTP, then terms. Also login, forgot/reset password, change password, log out and close account |
| **Profile** | Photo (gallery picker), mini bio and travel preferences (chattiness, music, smoking, pets). Experience levels run from Newcomer to Ambassador. There's a public profile page and a member-since date |
| **Verification** | Email code, phone OTP, and an ID upload (Aadhaar, DL, PAN, Passport, Voter ID) that earns a "Verified" badge |
| **Vehicles** | Add, edit and delete cars. The flow is plate → brand → model → body type → colour → year → fuel |
| **Car model database** | 33 brands and about 270 models for the Indian market (Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Toyota, Kia, MG, …), plus imports and an "other model" option |
| **City database** | About 150 Indian cities with coordinates. These drive the distance, duration and price estimates |
| **Exact pickup & drop-off** | "Use current location" (GPS) on *Leaving from*. After choosing a city, pick the exact point: a popular meeting point (with real coordinates), a typed address, your current location, or a pin dropped on the map |
| **Publish a ride** | From and to with exact pickup and drop-off points, suggested stopovers along the route, date and time, choice of car, seats, "Max. 2 in the back", women-only rides, Instant Booking or manual approval, a recommended price with limits, a comment, and an optional return trip |
| **Search** | Search by from, to, date and passengers, with recent searches and popular routes. Matching is segment-aware: a search can match a stopover, and the price is pro-rated for that part of the trip. Cities within 35 km also count, and the walking-distance indicator is measured from your exact pickup and drop-off points |
| **Results** | Sort by earliest, cheapest, closest or shortest. Filter by departure time, instant booking, max 2 in the back, verified drivers, women-only, smoking and pets. A date strip, suggested other dates, and ride alerts |
| **Ride details** | Itinerary timeline, OpenStreetMap route map, driver card with rating, car, preferences, passengers, contact driver, share and report |
| **Booking** | Choose seats and see the price breakdown with service fee. Pay by UPI, card (Luhn check) or cash to the driver. Instant booking or a request to book, with a confirmation screen and cancellation with refund |
| **Your rides** | Upcoming and past trips as driver and passenger, with status tags, pending-request badges and "rate" prompts |
| **Manage ride (driver)** | Accept or decline requests, see the passenger list, message passengers, edit the ride (passengers are notified if it's rescheduled), cancel (passengers are refunded and notified), share |
| **Inbox** | Chat per ride with unread badges, quick replies and a safety banner |
| **Ratings** | 1–5 stars with BlaBlaCar labels (Outstanding … Very disappointing) and a comment. Ratings received and given, with an average and a distribution chart |
| **Notifications** | In-app notification centre plus Android system notifications |
| **Ride alerts** | Saved searches. You're notified when new matching rides are published |
| **Payments** | Saved UPI IDs and cards, payout methods (UPI, or bank account with IFSC check), and payments, refunds and payouts history |
| **Other** | Notification, email and SMS settings, help centre FAQ, Terms, Privacy, Community guidelines, About, and dark mode |

## Technology

The app follows Google's recommended stack for a native Android app:

- **Kotlin 2.0** and **Jetpack Compose** with **Material 3**: one Activity, built entirely in Compose
- **Navigation Compose** for routing and **ViewModel + StateFlow** for state (MVVM)
- **Room** (SQLite) for storage, with code generated by KSP
- **DataStore** for the login session, **Coil** for images, **osmdroid** for OpenStreetMap maps (no API key needed)
- Platform `LocationManager` and `Geocoder` for current location and addresses (no Google Play services needed)
- AndroidX SplashScreen, edge-to-edge layout, and the Android 13+ notification permission
- **GitHub Actions** builds, signs and publishes the APK on every push (`.github/workflows/carsathi-android.yml`)

```
app/src/main/java/com/carsathi/app
├── data
│   ├── catalog     # City & car model databases, geo + pricing
│   ├── db          # Room entities, DAOs, database
│   ├── model       # UI/domain models (itinerary, segments, trips…)
│   ├── repo        # Auth, users, cars, rides & bookings, chat, ratings, alerts, wallet
│   └── seed        # Demo community seeder + simulator
├── notifications   # System notifications
└── ui              # Compose screens by feature + theme + shared components
```

## How data works in this build

All data is stored **on the device** (Room), so the APK works straight away without a server. On first launch the app seeds a community of 32 demo members with cars and ratings. It also keeps a rolling three-week schedule of rides on about 60 popular routes, such as Delhi–Jaipur, Mumbai–Pune and Bengaluru–Mysuru, so search returns results.

A small simulator makes that demo community respond while the app is open:

- Drivers reply to your messages.
- Drivers accept your booking requests.
- Passengers book the rides you publish.
- Members rate you back.
- ID checks get "approved".

OTP and email codes are shown on screen instead of being sent by SMS or email, and payments are simulated.

**Going live** means swapping the repositories in `AppContainer` (`CarSathiApp.kt`) for ones backed by a server. For example, Firebase Auth, Firestore and FCM, or Supabase, or your own API, plus an SMS/OTP provider and a payment gateway such as Razorpay. The UI and ViewModels don't need to change.

## Building locally

Install Android Studio (Ladybug or newer), or JDK 17 plus the Android SDK, then run:

```bash
cd carsathi
./gradlew assembleRelease          # → app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
```

Release builds are signed with the bundled **demo key** (`app/carsathi-demo.jks`), so every build installs over the previous one. Before publishing on Google Play:

1. Create your own upload key.
2. Set the `CARSATHI_KEYSTORE_FILE`, `CARSATHI_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`, `CARSATHI_KEY_ALIAS` and `CARSATHI_KEY_PASSWORD` environment variables (as CI secrets).
3. Remove the demo key.

## Logo

`branding/` holds a placeholder logo: a location pin over a car on brand blue. It comes as an SVG and a PNG, in both icon and wordmark versions. The same artwork is used as the adaptive launcher icon and the splash screen. To use your own logo, replace these files and `res/drawable/ic_launcher_*.xml`.
