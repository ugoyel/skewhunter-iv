Privacy Policy

CrossDeck plays videos through the embedded YouTube player and, if you add your own API key, searches videos through YouTube API Services. By using CrossDeck you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data from the player and the API is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What CrossDeck stores

Your track library, queue, mixer settings and optional API key are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. CrossDeck has no server and does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It does not download, record or store any audio or video from YouTube. Music files you open from your own device are played and analysed on the device only; they are never uploaded.

Third parties

The embedded YouTube player may set cookies and collect usage data under Google's policy above. Search requests go directly from your device to Google.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage (or uninstall the app) to delete everything CrossDeck has stored.
